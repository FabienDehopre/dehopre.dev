# dehopre.dev

Fabien Dehopré's personal website: who he is, what he has built, and what he works with.

## Language

**Project**:
A public GitHub repository that Fabien has chosen to show on the site. Repositories not on the curated list are not Projects.
_Avoid_: Repo (when meaning a showcased one), work, portfolio item. Not to be confused with an Nx project in the workspace.

**Role**:
A position Fabien held at a company over a period of time, shown in the Work section of the Home page.
_Avoid_: Job, experience, position

**Featured Project**:
A Project flagged to also appear on the Home page. At most three are shown.
_Avoid_: Highlighted project, pinned project (pinning is a GitHub profile feature, not this)

**Tool**:
Hardware, software or a service Fabien uses and recommends, listed on the Uses page.
_Avoid_: Gear, item, use

**Tool Category**:
A named group of Tools on the Uses page, such as "Workstation" or "Development tools".
_Avoid_: Section, group
